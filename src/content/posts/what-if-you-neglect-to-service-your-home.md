## What happens if you fail to wash your windows?

If you fail to wash your windows, dirt and grime will build up on the glass, making it difficult to see through. The build-up will also block out sunlight, making your home feel dark and dreary. In addition, the dirt and grime can damage the window, causing it to become cracked or warped.

### What if you neglect to clean your gutters?

If you neglect to clean your gutters, you may end up with a leaky roof or water damage to your home. Clogged gutters can also lead to ice dams in the winter, which can cause even more damage to your home. If you don’t want to clean your gutters yourself, you can hire a professional to do it for you.

### What if you do not wash your house siding?

If you don’t wash your siding, your house will start to look dingy and dirty. Moss and mildew will start to grow, and your paint will start to chip and peel. Your house will lose its curb appeal and will start to look run down.

### What if you avoid painting your house when the paint is old?

If you avoid painting your house when the paint is old, you may end up with a house that is not as well-protected from the elements as it could be. Paint protects your house from water damage, sun damage, and other types of wear and tear. If you wait too long to paint, you may find that your paint job does not last as long as it should, and you may have to repaint more often.

### What if you neglect to clean your automobile?

If you neglect to clean your automobile, it will eventually become covered in dirt and grime. This can cause the paint to become dull and the finish to start to deteriorate. The dirt and grime can also cause the wheels to become caked with build-up, making it difficult to turn. In extreme cases, the build-up can cause the engine to overheat. The car will also start to smell bad. If you never clean your car, it will eventually fall apart. Also, your friends and family will not like ride-sharing with you.
