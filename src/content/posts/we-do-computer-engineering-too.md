Most people find us because we wash their windows, clean their gutters or detail their cars. That's the side of the business you can see a truck doing. But it is not the only kind of work we do, and it is not where the company started technically. Alongside home maintenance, Specially Brilliant does computer engineering of all sorts of types.

I hold a degree in electrical engineering from Eastern Washington University, and the computer trade is where that training applies directly. Here is what that covers.

## Web design

Websites that load fast, work on a phone, and say honestly what a business does. That means the whole stack: layout and typography, structure that search engines can read, accessibility so the site works with a screen reader, and hosting set up so none of it falls over. This site is the proof of work — a static Astro build, no WordPress bloat, and every page written to answer a real question.

## Application design

Software for a specific job rather than a subscription that almost fits. Business-process tools, databases, dashboards, internal utilities, and mobile or desktop apps. The usual goal is to take something a person currently does by hand on a spreadsheet and make the machine do it, correctly, every time.

## Firmware design

Code that runs on hardware instead of on a laptop: microcontrollers, sensors, devices. Writing and updating firmware, debugging it when a device misbehaves, and taking a product idea from a breadboard prototype to something that can be built and flashed repeatably. Because the firmware touches the circuit, this work pairs naturally with the electronics side below.

## Electronics and embedded systems

Circuit design, PCB layout, component selection, and bench debugging. Where a project needs both hardware and code — a device that measures something, controls something, or reports over a network — one person can carry both halves instead of mediating between two vendors.

## Small-business IT and computer repair

Setting up computers and networks for a home office or a small shop: routing, Wi-Fi that reaches, shared storage, backups that actually run, email that doesn't land in spam, and repair or upgrade of the machines you already own. Plain advice about what to buy and what to skip.

## Bitcoin Cash integration

The one specialization most shops in this list won't offer. We are deep in Bitcoin Cash as a business [co-op member](/bitcoin-cash-co-op/), so we can wire a website or point of sale to accept BCH payments — setting up the wallet, the payment codes, and the settlement flow the same way we set up a website. If you want card-free payments without learning this from scratch, this is the trade to ask about.

## Why a window washer does this

Because the two halves of the business share one standard: measure carefully, quote honestly, and do the work so it doesn't come back. A badly routed network and a streaked window both get called back. We would rather do it right the first time, and we have the engineering background to say that about the computer work too.

All of this is available in the Spokane Valley and the surrounding area, in person or remotely.
