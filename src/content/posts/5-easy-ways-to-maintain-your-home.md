#### 4. Clean Regularly

It is very important in maintaining your homes’ environment by regular cleaning. If you are regularly cleaning the venting systems, that is great for your respiratory health. If you clean your carpets regularly, that is also great because it prevents harmful bacterial from spreading all throughout the home. By cleaning with a disinfectant, it will help to reduce the spread of bacteria. Also pay attention to reducing moisture in the home as moisture can induce the growth of mold. Mold can be dangerous to the lungs of people and animals. If mold does become a problem, then calling an expert is usually the best choice to remove it all.
