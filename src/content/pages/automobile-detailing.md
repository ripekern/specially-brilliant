## What our auto detailing includes

Detailing runs in three tiers — **Express**, **Detail** and **Full Detail** — so you pay for the level you actually want rather than for a package that over- or under-shoots. Cars and trucks, and we come to you.

The **Full Detail** is the complete job: exterior work plus interior vacuuming and cleaning, with dash and trim care. Wheels are cleaned, not just wiped.

We will tell you which tier suits your vehicle before anything starts, and the price is agreed in writing first.

## What is an add-on

Quoted separately from whichever tier you book, not assumed to be included:

- **Pet hair removal** — extra extraction for hair worked into carpets and upholstery
- **Odor and steam treatment** — for smoke, mildew or food odours
- **Headlight restore** — hazed or yellowed lenses made clear again
- **Ceramic or sealant coating** — longer-lasting protection than wax alone
- **Engine bay cleaning** — degreased and dressed

Price depends on the tier and the size and condition of the vehicle. Your written estimate carries the exact figure.

## When it needs doing

Once or twice a year for a daily-driven car, and more often for anything that sits outside through a Spokane or Coeur d'Alene winter. The timing that matters most is spring — after the salt is off the roads but before the pollen lands, which is the one window where both problems are not yet in the same place.

## Paint that holds its value

Auto detailing is crucial for car owners for several reasons. Firstly, regular detailing helps maintain the overall appearance and condition of the vehicle. This involves thorough cleaning, polishing, and waxing both the exterior and interior of the car, which not only enhances its aesthetic appeal but also preserves the paint and prevents corrosion.

## What detailing adds when you sell or trade

Secondly, auto detailing can increase the resale value of the car. A well-maintained vehicle with a clean and shiny exterior, spotless interior, and no unpleasant odors is more likely to fetch a higher price when it comes time to sell or trade it in.

## A cleaner car to drive, and cheaper than a new one

Lastly, auto detailing also contributes to the comfort and health of the driver and passengers. A clean interior free of dirt, dust, and allergens provides a more pleasant driving experience and reduces the risk of respiratory issues. Overall, investing in auto detailing is a wise decision for car owners looking to protect their investment, maintain their vehicle's value, and ensure a clean and safe driving environment.
