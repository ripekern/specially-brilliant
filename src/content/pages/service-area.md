## Where we work

We are based in Spokane and cover the whole corridor between Sandpoint, Coeur d'Alene and Airway Heights. It is the ground we can reach in a normal working day without the fuel costing more than the job earns, and after fourteen years of working it we know which roads wash out after heavy rain and which properties need a longer set-up than the square footage suggests.

## Spokane and the Valley

Spokane, Spokane Valley, Airway Heights, Cheney, Four Lakes, Medical Lake, Liberty Lake, Mead and Deer Park.

## North Idaho

Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls, Hayden, Hayden Lake, Athol, Spirit Lake, Priest River and Newman Lake.

## The far north

Sandpoint and Ponderay.

## Travel and estimates

Travel is built into the estimate rather than added on afterwards, so the price we quote is the price you pay. There is no separate fuel charge and no surcharge for coming out this far.

If you are just outside the towns listed here, ask anyway. We regularly take work further afield for larger jobs, and how far we will travel is usually decided by how much of the day the job takes rather than by the distance itself.
