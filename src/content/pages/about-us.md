## Hi — I'm the one who'll be at your door

![A man in a striped shirt and tie, holding a notepad and pen and smiling at the camera](/images/content/window-washer-in-spokane.webp)

Specially Brilliant is an owner-operated home maintenance business out of the Spokane Valley. The person who quotes your job is the person who does it: no handoffs, no mystery crews, and you deal with the same person from the estimate to the final walkthrough.

## Where I started

I grew up in Newport, a small town north of Spokane, where my family managed a 40-acre mountain farm and cared for a diverse range of livestock. This upbringing instilled in me a deep appreciation for hard work and responsibility from an early age.

## Learning the trade

Under my father's guidance, I developed a strong work ethic while assisting in home construction and remodeling projects, focusing primarily on site preparation and cleanup. I later earned a degree in Electrical Engineering from Eastern Washington University, where I also took an active role in building community within campus housing.

That degree still earns its keep. Alongside the maintenance work, Specially Brilliant does [computer engineering of all sorts of types](/journal/we-do-computer-engineering-too/) — web design, application design, firmware design, and small-business IT.

## Running Specially Brilliant

![The same man seated, looking down as he writes in a notepad](/images/content/expert-home-services-in-spokane.webp)

We have been meeting the needs of residential customers since 2012: [window washing](/window-washing/), [gutter cleaning](/gutter-cleaning/), [pressure washing](/pressure-washing/), [house painting](/house-painting/), [automobile detailing](/automobile-detailing/) and [roof moss removal](/moss-removal/). Our goal is to provide the best possible service, and we are always looking for ways to improve; the feedback customers have given over the years has genuinely made the business better.

Most of what we do is prevention — small, regular care that keeps a house out of the expensive trouble it drifts into when it is ignored. Two journal posts cover exactly that: [What if you neglect to service your home?](/journal/what-if-you-neglect-to-service-your-home/) and [Some easy ways to maintain your home](/journal/5-easy-ways-to-maintain-your-home/).

I am also pursuing studies in biblical law and government alongside the business. That combination of entrepreneurship and lifelong learning reflects a commitment to both professional excellence and meaningful service.

## Outside the work

I have a passion for outdoor activities such as skiing, boating, swimming, and hiking, and I keep an active routine at the fitness center — basketball, volleyball, racquetball, and running, with dedicated time for stretching and recovery. I follow a clean diet, reflecting a focus on health and wellness.

Beyond physical activity, I enjoy traveling, connecting with others, and creative outlets including writing, reading, calligraphy, graphic design, web design, and painting. Teaching, cleaning, and continuous learning are also integral parts of my life.

## Bitcoin Cash

I am a strong advocate for Bitcoin Cash and am passionate about helping people understand and use it. I believe that as more people adopt Bitcoin Cash, the world becomes a more just, free and empowered place for everyone. We accept it for every service, and we're working with other local businesses to build a co-op around it — the reasoning is in [Why We Joined a Bitcoin Cash Co-Op](/journal/why-we-joined-a-bitcoin-cash-co-op/).
