These are reviews left on our Google Business Profile. They are here in the
reviewer's own words, exactly as written — shortened only where a name was
replaced with a first name and last initial.

## What people actually notice

The reviews are short, and they keep saying much the same thing: the work is done
properly, and it is done by the person you speak to on the phone. One of them
mentions coming back for years rather than calling once — which is the part a new
customer cannot check for themselves, and the reason we have put these reviews
here instead of only on Google.

Not every review has words in it. One of these is a rating with no comment, and it
counts the same as the rest.

## How we got here

Sven has been owner-operating in the Spokane Valley since 2012. Most of the
business still comes from people he has already worked for, which is why the
reviews above mention second and third visits rather than a first impression.

If you have already had work done and have a minute to spare, a review on Google
is the most useful thing you could leave. It is the only place a prospective
customer can check what we say about ourselves.
