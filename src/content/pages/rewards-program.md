This program is our way of saying thank you for being a customer, while also helping expand the use and awareness of Bitcoin Cash as a fast, low-fee, peer-to-peer currency. Each BCH paper wallet serves as both a reward and a tool for onboarding—easy to use, easy to share, and always valuable.
