## Pay by card

:::callout
**Card payments are not available on this page yet.** The WordPress site used a
WP Easy Pay form here; it could not be carried over to a static site, because
card details must be submitted over a secure connection to a payment processor
and never stored as part of a page.

To pay by card, [book your visit](https://calendar.proton.me/bookings#kqhaJrYVSePagYuW_pmFqPAoyub9f4pgF9w4Ps5jiMA=)
and mention that you would like an invoice sent. You will receive a secure
payment link by email.
:::

**Authorization**

By completing this form, you authorize Specially Brilliant to charge your bank
card for the total amount entered, which requires a **4% bank card processing
fee**. This fee is applied to cover banking network processing fees and delays. By
proceeding, you acknowledge and agree to this charge in addition to the base amount
for services rendered, and that we do not guarantee any security of sending bank
card information.
