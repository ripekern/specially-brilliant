## Prefer to talk it through?

Some jobs are easier to scope by phone than through a calendar, especially if you
are not sure which service you need. Describe what you are after and you will get
a straight answer on cost and timing.

## Before you book

Photos help more than you would think. If you have a picture of the window,
the siding, or the area that needs attention, send it over and the estimate will
be closer to the real thing.
